Before using a form

Forms are provided for general convenience and informational purposes. Elite Empire Notary is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. These forms may not meet every legal, agency, airline, or destination requirement. Confirm the required form and wording with the receiving organization or a licensed attorney before use. Providing a form does not guarantee acceptance or legal sufficiency.

Prepare for your appointment

Complete the applicable information, but leave signature lines and all notary certificate fields blank until your appointment. If notarization is requested, bring acceptable identification.
